Aeronautical Glossary

Key terms and definitions used in the industry.

Aerodynamics
Study of the forces and motion of objects through the air.
Fuselage
Main body of an aircraft that holds crew, passengers, and cargo.
Lift
Force that acts perpendicular to the direction of motion and keeps the aircraft in the air.
