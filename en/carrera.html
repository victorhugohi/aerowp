Aeronautics Career (UMSA)

The Aeronautics Career at the Higher University of San Andrés (UMSA) trains professionals skilled in the design, maintenance, and management of aeronautical systems.

Professional Profile

Graduates are prepared to face the challenges of the modern aviation industry, with solid knowledge in engineering, mechanics, and international regulations.

Areas of Study

  • Aerodynamics and Flight Mechanics
  • Aeronautical Structures and Materials
  • Propulsion Systems
  • Maintenance and Air Safety

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long is the program?

The career lasts approximately 5 years.

What are the admission requirements?

A high school diploma is required, along with passing the entrance exam or pre-university course.

Where can I work after graduating?

In airlines, maintenance workshops, airport administration, and regulatory bodies.
